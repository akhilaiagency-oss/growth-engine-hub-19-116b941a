## Recommended target count: 10–11 sections

A discovery-call landing page has one job: turn intent into a booked ₹9 call. Every section must either (a) establish the problem, (b) prove the mechanism, (c) remove risk, or (d) drive the click. Sections that repeat the same job create scroll fatigue and dilute urgency.

### Keep as standalone (non-negotiable)

- **Hero** — first impression + primary CTA.
- **Leak Map** — proprietary diagnostic, tied to nav link.
- **Engine** — proprietary mechanism, tied to nav link.
- **Session / Offer** — the offer itself, price anchor, risk reversal.
- **How It Works** — reduces uncertainty before payment, tied to nav link.
- **FAQ** — objection handling, tied to nav link.
- **Final CTA** — close the loop.

### Merge or remove

| Section | Current role | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Trust Strip** | Social proof placeholder | **Remove or collapse into Hero.** Without real logos/testimonials it adds no trust and creates a credibility gap. |
| **Problem** | Agitate pain | **Keep, but tighten.** This is the setup for the Leak Map. |
| **Already Paying** | Expand problem | **Merge into Problem.** It is the same point: "you are spending but leaking leads." |
| **Future Pacing** | Paint success | **Merge into Outcomes.** Both show the post-call state; one is enough. |
| **Outcomes** | Specific deliverables | **Keep,** but absorb Future Pacing. |
| **Proof** | Honest credibility | **Keep,** but keep it short. The "Built To Be Measured" block is strong. |
| **Founder** | Personal trust | **Keep,** but place it close to the offer or after proof, not as a standalone hero. |
| **Why Us** | Differentiation | **Merge into Engine or How It Works.** The Engine already differentiates; "Why Us" restates it. |
| **Who It's For** | Qualification | **Merge into Problem or Session.** Qualify early in the problem section and reinforce at the offer. |

### Proposed final flow (11 sections)

1. Hero
2. Problem (absorbs Already Paying + Who It's For)
3. Leak Map
4. Engine (absorbs Why Us)
5. Outcomes (absorbs Future Pacing)
6. Proof
7. Founder
8. Session / Offer (absorbs Who It's For reinforcement)
9. How It Works
10. FAQ
11. Final CTA

### Optional aggressive cut (10 sections)

Move **Founder** into the **Session / Offer** card as a small signature block, removing the standalone founder section. This works only if the founder photo and quote can be compressed without losing trust.
